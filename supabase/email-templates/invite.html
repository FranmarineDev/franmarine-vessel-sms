From Tuesday 3 November 2026 the vessel Safety Management System moves off paper and MS Forms into one Franmarine app. Everything you currently sign on the boat — induction, daily sign-on, pre-departure checks, drill records — is done in the app on your phone, and it keeps the records AMSA expects under MO504 without anyone chasing forms.

Getting set up — 5 minutes, before your next day on the water

  1. Tap the button above and set a password. Your region and role are already set.
  2. Open {{ .SiteURL }} and sign in. Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) so it sits with your other apps.
  3. Tap each vessel you operate and complete its induction — 11 short screens of that vessel's own SMS content: safety gear locations, VHF channels, limits, assembly station. About 5 minutes per vessel, once a year. Best done at the yard, not on the wharf at 0600.

Every day you're master

  • Tap the vessel → Sign on, choose Master, confirm the fitness-for-work declarations. Under a minute.
  • Complete the pre-departure checks before leaving the berth — the 05A checklist, OK / Fail / N/A per item. A critical fail shows DO NOT DEPART. Same rule as the paper version; the app just records it.
  • Check Today's crew against your verbal roll call. Anyone not on that list hasn't signed on.
  • Crew sign on from their own phones. Visitors, client reps and anyone without an account scan the QR at the helm, or you add them from the crew list on your phone.
  • Record drills in the app as you run them. The panel shows what's due — fire and MOB monthly; medical, abandon and flooding quarterly; diving before each campaign.

If you can't tick something

Don't. If you haven't had your rest, or an item fails, the app won't let you sign through it — talk to Kirby or Matt. That's the system working, not breaking.

For the first few weeks keep the paper logbook going alongside it. Once we're confident, paper goes.

Any problems, call Kirby Hamilton or Matt Hilder. Feedback welcome — this is version one and you're the people who'll find what's clunky.
